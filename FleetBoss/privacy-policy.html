Fleet Boss – Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 16 November 2026 · Applies to the Fleet Boss: Idle Taxi & Bus game for Android

In short:
  • Fleet Boss has no account and no sign-in. Your company, coins, gems and settings are saved on your device only. We do not run any server that receives information about you.
  • The game is free. It shows only rewarded ads that you choose to watch for a bonus. There are no ad breaks and no banners. Ads come from our advertising partners through Unity LevelPlay.
  • Purchases (the Starter pack, the Ad-free pass, 2× fares forever and gem packs) go through Google Play. We never see card or bank details.
  • The game is for players aged 13 and over.
  • We do not sell your information, and we do not use analytics, location, contacts, camera, microphone or notifications.

2. Who the game is for

Fleet Boss is an idle taxi and bus tycoon game for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not designed for or directed at children under 13, and our advertising partners are told that the game is not child-directed. If you are under 13, please do not use the game. If you believe a child under 13 is using it, contact us and we will help remove the game's data from their device.

3. Information saved on your device

Fleet Boss has no account. The game saves the following on your device only. None of it is sent to us:

WhatWhy
Game progress: coins, gems, vehicles, licences, upgrades, districts, dispatchers, paints, quests, achievements, the collection book, the daily reward streak and your records (such as passengers this week and fastest bus licence)To keep your progress between sessions
The time you last playedTo work out what your company earned while you were away

Bonus counters: when each ad bonus was last used, how many were used today, and whether you chose gems instead of adsTo apply the bonus rules in section 6

You can remove all of it by deleting the game, or by clearing its storage in your device settings. There is no cloud save, so this also removes your progress. Items bought for good can be restored afterwards (section 7).

4. Your advertising choices

  • No ad plays unless you tap for it. You can also turn on Gems instead of ads in the Bonus tab and never see an ad, or buy the Ad-free pass.
  • Personalised ads. On Android you can delete or reset your advertising ID in Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads (or Settings ▸ Google ▸ Ads). Without it, our partners show only non-personalised ads.
  • Europe. The game does not yet show a consent form. Until it does, it is not offered in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland. Before it is, we will add Google's consent form, where you can accept or refuse personalised advertising and change your choice at any time in the game's Settings, and we will update this policy first.

5. Advertising

Fleet Boss is free because it offers ads. We use Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies, which includes ironSource) to request ads from advertising networks. The networks currently used are ironSource Ads, Unity Ads and Google AdMob. If we add another network, we will list it here first.

When an ad is requested or shown, the ad SDKs in the game collect information directly from your device. We do not receive this information ourselves. It can include:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID), unless you deleted it;
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location (country or city, not precise location);
  • device and app information: device model, operating system and version, language, time zone, network type, screen size and the game's version;
  • ad interactions: which ads were shown, whether you watched them to the end or tapped them, and whether a reward was given.

Our partners use this information to deliver ads, to limit how often you see the same ad, to measure ads, to prevent fraud and to show ads based on your interests where that is allowed. Partners use the information under their own privacy policies, and some act as independent controllers of it:

PartnerWhat it doesPrivacy policy
Unity LevelPlay, ironSource Ads and Unity Ads (Unity Technologies)Ad mediation and the ads you seeUnity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy
Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK)Ads you seeGoogle Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps

Unity Ads also lets you opt out of personalised ads through the privacy icon shown on its ads.

6. How ads are shown

  • Every ad is your choice. An ad plays only when you tap a bonus: 2× earnings for a while, a free crate, a longer rush hour, finishing a long trip now, or doubling what your company earned while you were away. You never have to watch one to play.
  • No ad breaks, no banners, and nothing plays by itself.
  • Gems instead of ads: a switch in the Bonus tab lets you pay a few gems for a bonus instead of watching an ad.
  • Ad-free pass: a one-time purchase that gives every bonus without an ad.

7. Purchases

You can buy the Starter pack, the Ad-free pass, 2× fares forever and gem packs. Purchases are made and paid through Google Play, which handles payment and holds your payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive only a confirmation of the purchase (product, order ID, date), and the game records on your device what you bought. We never see your name, card or bank details. The Starter pack, the Ad-free pass and 2× fares forever come back with Settings ▸ Restore purchases after you reinstall the game or move to a new phone with the same Google account.

8. Crash reports and the game engine

The game contains no analytics or crash-reporting software of its own. If it crashes, Google Play (through "Android vitals") may give us a crash report, but only if you have chosen, in your device's settings, to share usage and diagnostics data. These reports reach us anonymised and contain technical details such as the device model, operating system version and the part of the game that failed. We use them only to fix problems.

The game is built with the Unity engine, which may send limited technical device information (such as device model, operating system, graphics hardware and screen size) to Unity to improve its engine. It does not include your name, contact details or precise location. Unity privacy policy

9. What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location.
  • We do not use usage analytics, social media features or our own tracking.
  • We do not sell your personal information. Sharing information with ad partners for personalised ads may count as "sharing" under some US state laws; you can stop it as described in sections 4 and 11.
  • We do not use notifications.

10. Where information goes, and how long it is kept

  • On your device: until you delete the game or clear its storage.
  • With advertising partners and Google: processed on their servers, which may be outside your country, including in the United States, and kept for the periods set out in their privacy policies. Where information leaves a region with transfer rules, these companies use the legal safeguards those rules require, such as Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • With us: nothing, apart from anonymised crash reports (section 8) and any emails you send us, which we keep only as long as needed to answer them.

11. Your rights, and the laws we follow

Depending on where you live, you have some or all of these rights: to access, correct or delete your information, to receive a copy, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because the game stores your information only on your device, you can delete it yourself by deleting the game or clearing its storage. To stop personalised ads, delete your advertising ID in your device's ad settings (section 4). For information held by an advertising partner, you can also contact that partner through the links in section 5. If you need help, email us. We answer within 30 days and never charge for a reasonable request.

  • United States – COPPA and state privacy laws. The game is not directed at children under 13 (section 2). Residents of some states (for example California) can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising by deleting their advertising ID in their device's ad settings, or by emailing us.
  • India – the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. You may raise a grievance with our Grievance Officer (section 1) and then with the Data Protection Board of India.
  • European Union, United Kingdom and Switzerland – GDPR, UK GDPR and the Swiss FADP. The game is not offered there yet (section 4). When it is, personalised advertising will be based on your consent, and non-personalised ads on our legitimate interest in funding a free game. You may complain to your local data protection authority.
  • Canada – PIPEDA and Australia – the Privacy Act 1988, and other applicable laws.

If we change how the game handles information, for example by adding a consent form, cloud save, sign-in, online leaderboards or another advertising network, we will update this policy and the date at the top before the change takes effect, and ask for consent where the law requires it.
