Fleet Boss – Delete Your Data

Last updated: 18 November 2026 · Fleet Boss: Idle Taxi & Bus, by AJAS M M (Paakkaan Studio)

You can delete your Fleet Boss data at any time. Fleet Boss has no account of its own and we keep no player data on our own servers. Your game is saved on your device and, if you sign in with Google Play Games, a copy is kept in your Google account. The options below remove each of them. You do not need the game installed to ask us by email.

Option 1: delete the cloud save in the game

  1. Open Fleet Boss and tap the gear button to open Settings.
  2. Find Delete cloud save and tap Delete….
  3. Tap Delete again to confirm.

This removes your company from Google Play straight away and turns cloud saving off. The game on this phone keeps working. Cloud saving stays off until you tap Save now in Settings.

Option 2: delete the game data on your device

  • Uninstall Fleet Boss, or
  • open your device's Settings ▸ Apps ▸ Fleet Boss ▸ Storage and tap Clear storage.

This removes your progress, settings and records from the device. If your cloud save still exists, the game restores it the next time you install it and sign in to Google Play Games, so use Option 1 or 3 first if you want everything gone.

Option 3: delete it through Google Play Games

Your cloud save, achievements and leaderboard scores are held by Google Play Games in your Google account. To remove all of Fleet Boss's data there:

  1. Open the Google Play Games app (or the Play Games section of the Play Store) and go to its Settings.
  2. Choose Delete Play Games account & data, then Delete individual game data.
  3. Find Fleet Boss and delete it.

Google handles this deletion under the Google Privacy Policy.

Option 4: ask us by email

Email paakkaanstudio@gmail.com with the subject "Fleet Boss data deletion". Tell us what you want deleted and we will walk you through it, or delete any information we hold about you, such as your emails to us. We answer within 30 days and never charge for this.
