Fleet Boss
Idle Taxi & Bus

Fleet Boss is an idle taxi & bus tycoon where you run a city's transport company. Tap fares, hire dispatchers, grow your fleet and earn while you're away.

Coming soonGoogle Play

Features

Build your transport empire

  • Start with a single bike, then unlock auto-rickshaws, cabs, family cars, luxury cars, city buses and luxury coaches.
  • Every vehicle has its own passengers: school runs, office buses, airport trips, beach tours.
  • Expand your garage and fill the roads with your fleet.

Grow the city

  • Unlock new districts one by one: the airport, the beach, the business district, the college and the hills.
  • Reach passenger milestones to add new landmarks to your city.
  • Rush hours bring crowds and double fares.

Idle and offline earnings

  • Hire dispatchers who take trips for you.
  • Your company keeps earning while you're away.
  • Upgrade speed, fares and capacity to grow faster.

Collect and compete

  • Daily quests, a daily reward and dozens of achievements.
  • Paint your vehicles, switch on night lights and complete the collection book.
  • Beat your records and climb the Google Play Games leaderboards.

Played your way

  • No forced ads, ever. Ads only play when you choose a bonus.
  • Relaxing idle play: tap when you want, earn when you don't.
  • Sign in with Google Play Games to keep your company safe on a new phone.
