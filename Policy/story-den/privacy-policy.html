Story Den – Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 4 October 2026 · Applies to the Story Den app

In short:
  • Story Den is for adults (18 or over). You can read without an account.
  • An account is optional. It uses your email address to keep your bookmarks, reading history and coins safe across devices.
  • The app shows rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one, through LevelPlay (Unity). Ad networks receive your device's advertising ID and technical details to show and measure ads.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, we ask for your consent before ads are personalised, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in Reading preferences ▸ Privacy choices.
  • We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used, as far as your consent allows.
  • Coins are bought through Google Play. We never see card or bank details.
  • You can delete the account and all its data at any time, in the app or by emailing us.

2. Who the app is for

Story Den is an illustrated, interactive storybook for adults aged 18 or over (or the age of majority where you live). It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

4.2 Signing out

When you sign out, the app first saves your bookmarks, reading history and coins to the account, then removes them from the device, so the next person to use the device does not see them. Signing in again brings them back.

5. Coins and purchases

Story Den uses coins to unlock some screens of some stories. You can get coins in two ways:

  • Buying coin packs through Google Play. Google handles the payment and holds your payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive only a purchase confirmation (product, order ID, date), which the app checks against Google Play's signature before crediting the coins.
  • Watching a rewarded ad, when you choose to (section 6).

Coins and unlocked screens are recorded on your device and, if you have an account, against your account.

6. Advertising

Story Den shows rewarded ads only: a video you choose to watch in exchange for coins. There are no banners, and no ads that interrupt your reading.

Ads are delivered by LevelPlay, an ad mediation service by Unity Technologies (formerly ironSource). LevelPlay picks an ad from one of the ad networks it works with, such as Unity Ads. To request, show, measure and protect ads against fraud, LevelPlay and the ad networks receive:

  • your device's advertising ID (the Android Advertising ID);
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it (country or city, never precise location);
  • device and app information: device model, operating system and version, language, screen size, network type, app version;
  • ad interactions: which ads were shown, watched to the end, skipped or tapped.

Where you have consented (section 7), or where the law does not require consent, ad networks may use this information to show ads based on your interests, and may combine it with information they collect in other apps and websites, under their own privacy policies. Without that consent, ads are not personalised, and the information is used only to deliver, limit, measure and protect ads.

Your controls:

  • Change your consent at any time in Reading preferences ▸ Privacy choices (shown where the law requires it).
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (on Android: Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads, or Settings ▸ Google ▸ Ads). Ad networks then cannot link new ads to your past activity.
  • You never have to watch an ad. The app works without it.

7. Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your choices before any ad is requested, using Google's User Messaging Platform, a consent form certified under the IAB Europe Transparency and Consent Framework. Your choices are saved on your device and passed to LevelPlay and the ad networks, and they also apply to analytics (section 8). To decide whether the form is needed and to store your choices, Google receives your IP address and device information, under the Google Privacy Policy. The Google software that shows this form is used only for the form. Google does not serve ads in Story Den.

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in Reading preferences ▸ Privacy choices. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before.

8. Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how Story Den is used, so we can improve it: for example which stories are opened, where readers stop, whether the shop and ads work, and how many people use the app each day. It records events such as opening the app, opening a story, unlocking a screen, buying coins and watching an ad, together with an app instance ID, device and app information, and approximate location derived from the IP address. We do not send your email address or story text to Analytics.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, analytics follows your choices in the consent form (section 7). You can change them in Reading preferences ▸ Privacy choices.

9. Crash reports

The app contains no crash-reporting software of its own. If it crashes, Google Play (through "Android vitals") may give us a crash report, but only if you have chosen, in your device's settings, to share usage and diagnostics data with app developers. These reports reach us anonymised and contain technical details such as the device model, operating system version and the part of the app that failed. We use them only to fix problems.

11. Where the information is stored

Account and analytics information is stored on Google Cloud servers, which may be outside your country, including in the United States or the European Union. Ad networks may process information in the United States and other countries. Where information is transferred out of the EU, the UK or another region with transfer rules, our providers use the legal safeguards those rules require, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. All information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

12. How long we keep it

  • Account, bookmarks, reading history and coins: for as long as the account exists. If you delete the account, we delete this information within 30 days. If an account is not signed in to for 24 months, we delete it and its data.
  • Purchase records: for as long as the account exists, and afterwards only as long as tax, accounting or consumer law requires.
  • Analytics data: kept by Google Analytics for 14 months, then deleted.
  • Advertising data: kept by LevelPlay and the ad networks under their own retention rules.
  • Sign-in security information: kept by Firebase for a limited period under its own retention rules.
  • Data only on your device: until you delete the app or its storage.

13. How we protect it

Access to account data is restricted by security rules so that each account can read and change only its own data. Only verified email addresses can store data online. Passwords are never stored in readable form. Purchases are checked against Google Play's signature before coins are credited. Access to our Firebase console is limited to the developer and protected with 2-step verification.

14. Stories made with AI

Some or all of the words and pictures in Story Den's stories are created with the help of artificial-intelligence tools and reviewed before publishing. Translations may contain mistakes. AI tools are used only to make the stories. Nothing you do in the app is sent to any AI service.

15. Your rights, and the laws we follow

Depending on where you live, you have some or all of these rights: to access your information, correct it, delete it, receive a copy in a portable format, object to or restrict its use, and withdraw consent at any time. To use any of them, use the app (account deletion, Privacy choices) or email us. We answer within 30 days and never charge for a reasonable request. We may need to confirm you own the account first.

  • European Union, United Kingdom and Switzerland – GDPR, UK GDPR and the Swiss FADP. Our legal bases are listed in section 4.1. Personalised advertising and analytics rely on your consent (section 7). You may complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).
  • United States – state privacy laws (for example California's CCPA/CPRA). Sharing your advertising ID and ad interactions with ad networks for personalised advertising may count as "selling" or "sharing" personal information under these laws. You can opt out by resetting or deleting your advertising ID in your device settings (section 6), or by emailing us. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16.
  • Canada – PIPEDA and applicable provincial laws.
  • Australia – the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles. You may complain to the OAIC.
  • India – the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. You may raise a grievance with our Grievance Officer (section 1) and then with the Data Protection Board of India.
