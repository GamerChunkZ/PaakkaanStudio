Story Den – Delete Your Account

Last updated: 4 October 2026 · Story Den, by AJAS M M (Paakkaan Studio)

You can delete your Story Den account and its data at any time, in the app or by email. You do not need the app installed to ask by email. Story Den keeps working without an account.

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open Story Den and tap Library card on the menu.
  2. Sign in if you are not signed in already.
  3. Type your password in Password to cancel, then tap Cancel the card and confirm.

This deletes the account and its online data straight away, and also clears your bookmarks, coins and unlocked screens from that device.

Option 2: by email

Send an email to gamerchunkz@gmail.com with:

  • the subject "Story Den account deletion";
  • the email address you use to sign in to Story Den.

Email us now

Please send it from that same email address, so we know the request comes from the account holder. If it cannot come from that address, we will email the account address to confirm before deleting anything. We never ask for your password.

We delete the account within 30 days and email you when it is done.

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Your email address and passwordDeleted
Your account IDDeleted
Online bookmarks, reading progress and reading historyDeleted
Your coins and unlocked screensDeleted. Coins have no cash value and are not refunded.
Coin purchase records (product, order ID, date; never card details)Kept only as long as tax, accounting or consumer law requires, then deleted. They are no longer linked to a usable account.
Sign-in security logs held by Firebase (IP address, device, time)Expire automatically under Google Firebase's own retention rules

Analytics and advertising data are not linked to your account: they use your device's app instance ID and advertising ID. Analytics data is deleted automatically after 14 months. To stop new advertising data being linked to you, reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings, and change your choices in Reading preferences ▸ Privacy choices where shown.

If you used Story Den without an account, there is no account to delete: everything is on your device. Delete the app, or clear its storage in your device settings.

Refunds for coin purchases are handled by the store you bought from.

Only want some data deleted?

Email us at the same address and tell us what to remove, for example your reading history, and we will delete just that within 30 days.
