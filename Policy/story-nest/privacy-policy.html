Story Nest – Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 29 September 2026 · Applies to the Story Nest app for Android and iOS

In short, for parents:
  • Children can read Story Nest without any account. Without an account, nothing about your child or you leaves the device.
  • An account is optional and for grown-ups only. A parent or guardian creates it, behind a parental gate, with their own email address. It saves bookmarks online so they follow your family to a new device, and it keeps any purchases you make.
  • We never ask a child for their name, email, photo, voice or location. There are no ads, no usage analytics, no tracking and no selling or sharing of data.
  • If the app crashes, Google Play or the App Store may give us an anonymous crash report, but only if you have agreed on your device to share diagnostics with app developers.
  • "New story" notifications are off until a grown-up turns them on behind the parental gate. They only ever announce new or updated stories: never ads, never reminders to keep playing.
  • Purchases are made through Google Play or the App Store, behind the parental gate. We never see card or bank details.
  • You can delete the account and all its data at any time, in the app or by emailing us.

2. Who the app is for

Story Nest is an illustrated, interactive storybook for children aged 3 to 12, to be used with a parent or guardian. Accounts and purchases are for adults only (18 or over, or the age of majority where you live). They sit behind a parental gate: a task a young child cannot easily complete.

4.2 Parental rights (including under COPPA)

As the account holder you can at any time:

  • review the information held about your family: the bookmarks are shown in the app, and you can email us for a full copy;
  • correct your email address;
  • delete the account and all its data in the app (Library card ▸ Delete) or by emailing us;
  • withdraw consent and stop any further collection by deleting the account. The app keeps working without one.

5. Purchases

Some stories or chapters may be offered for purchase. Purchases:

  • are available only to a signed-in account and only after the parental gate, and always show the real price before you confirm;
  • are paid through Google Play or the Apple App Store. The store handles payment and holds your payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive only a purchase confirmation (product, order or transaction ID, date) to check with the store that the purchase is genuine;
  • are recorded against your account so they can be restored on another device, and updated if the store tells us a purchase was refunded.

We recommend using the store's own parental controls as well: Google Play Family Link and purchase approval, or Apple's Ask to Buy.

5a. Crash reports

The app contains no crash-reporting software of its own. If it crashes, Google Play (through "Android vitals") or Apple (through App Store Connect) may give us a crash report, but only if you have chosen, in your device's settings, to share usage and diagnostics data with app developers. These reports are collected by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies. They reach us anonymised and contain technical details such as the device model, operating system version and the part of the app that failed. We use them only to fix problems. You can switch this sharing off at any time in your device's settings.

5b. "New story" notifications

Story Nest can tell your family when new stories are added. Notifications are:

  • off by default. Only a grown-up can turn them on, in Settings behind the parental gate, and the device will also ask for permission;
  • only about stories ("A new story has arrived"). We never use notifications for advertising, and never to push a child to keep playing or to come back;
  • sent to everyone who turned them on at once, not to individuals. We do not know who receives them.

To deliver them, Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) gives the app installation a random notification token and uses it to route messages to the device. We do not store the token or link it to an account. Turning notifications off in the app, or in the device settings, stops them. Turning them off in the app also removes the token. Unused tokens expire automatically.

7. Where the information is stored

Account information is stored on Google Cloud servers used by Firebase, which may be located outside your country, including in the United States or the European Union. Where information is transferred out of the EU, the UK or another region with transfer rules, our providers use the legal safeguards those rules require, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. All information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

8. How long we keep it

  • Account, bookmarks and reading progress: for as long as the account exists. If you delete the account, we delete this information within 30 days. If an account is not signed in to for 24 months, we delete it and its data.
  • Purchase records: for as long as the account exists, and afterwards only as long as tax, accounting or consumer law requires, and then deleted.
  • Sign-in security information: kept by Firebase for a limited period under its own retention rules.
  • Notification token: until notifications are turned off or the token expires from lack of use.
  • Data only on your device: until you delete the app or its storage.

9. How we protect it

Access to account data is restricted by security rules so that each account can read and change only its own data. Only verified email addresses can store data online. Passwords are never stored in readable form. Purchases are checked on our server with the store, never trusted from the device alone. Access to our Firebase console is limited to the developer and protected with 2-step verification.

10. Stories made with AI

Some or all of the words and pictures in Story Nest's stories are created with the help of artificial-intelligence tools and reviewed before publishing. Translations may contain mistakes. AI tools are used only to make the stories. Nothing your child does in the app is sent to any AI service.

11. Your rights, and the laws we follow

Depending on where you live, you have some or all of these rights: to access your information, correct it, delete it, receive a copy in a portable format, object to or restrict its use, and withdraw consent at any time. To use any of them, delete the account in the app or email us. We answer within 30 days and never charge for a reasonable request. We may need to confirm you own the account first.

  • United States – COPPA. The account is created by a parent, who gives consent by creating it after the parental gate and confirming their email. The information is used only to provide the app to your family and is never disclosed for other purposes. The notification token is used only to deliver the notifications a parent turned on, never for advertising or to build a profile. Parents may review, delete and stop further collection as described in section 4.2. Residents of some US states (for example California) have additional rights under state privacy laws; we do not sell or share personal information.
  • European Union and United Kingdom – GDPR, UK GDPR and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code. Our legal bases are listed in section 4.1, plus consent for notifications. Settings are private by default, notifications are off by default, the child is never profiled, and nothing nudges a child to share data or to keep using the app. You may complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).
  • Canada – PIPEDA and applicable provincial laws.
  • Australia – the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles. You may complain to the OAIC.
  • India – the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Personal data relating to a child is processed only with the verifiable consent of a parent (the account holder), with no tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted advertising. You may raise a grievance with our Grievance Officer (section 1) and then with the Data Protection Board of India.
