Story Nest – Delete Your Account

Last updated: 29 September 2026 · Story Nest for Android and iOS, by AJAS M M (Paakkaan Studio)

You can delete your Story Nest account and its data at any time, in the app or by email. You do not need the app installed to ask by email. Story Nest keeps working without an account.

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open Story Nest and pass the grown-up check (the parental gate).
  2. Sign in, then open the account on the Library card.
  3. Choose Delete and confirm.

This deletes the account and its online data.

Option 2: by email

Send an email to gamerchunkz@gmail.com with:

  • the subject "Story Nest account deletion";
  • the email address you use to sign in to Story Nest.

Email us now

Please send it from that same email address, so we know the request comes from the account holder. If it cannot come from that address, we will email the account address to confirm before deleting anything. We never ask for your password.

We delete the account within 30 days and email you when it is done.

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Your email address and passwordDeleted
Your account IDDeleted
Online bookmarks and reading progressDeleted
Purchase records (product, order ID, date; never card details)Kept only as long as tax, accounting or consumer law requires, then deleted. They are no longer linked to a usable account.
Sign-in security logs held by Firebase (IP address, device, time)Expire automatically under Google Firebase's own retention rules

Deleting the account does not remove what is saved on the device itself. To remove that, delete the app or clear its storage in your device settings.

Purchases made through Google Play or the App Store cannot be restored once the account is deleted. Refunds are handled by the store you bought from.

Only want some data deleted?

Email us at the same address and tell us what to remove, for example your reading progress, and we will delete just that within 30 days.
