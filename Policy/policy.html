2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

To provide and improve our Service, we collect certain information, either directly from your device or through third-party services.

A. Data Collected Directly by the Developer

We collect specific data to optimize game performance, ensure stability, track bugs, and prevent fraud. This includes:

  • Device Information: Device manufacturer and model, processor (CPU) details and core count, available and total memory (RAM) size, and Operating System version.

  • Custom Identifiers: A Globally Unique Identifier (GUID) generated at runtime to help us identify and fix bugs early.

  • Purchase Verification: While we do not process payments, we collect and store the order receipt ID associated with in-app purchases solely for the purpose of preventing fraud and tampering.

  • Location Data: We do not directly collect, request, or store your precise GPS location data. (Note: Third-party ad networks may use your IP address to infer approximate location for ad serving).

B. User Accounts and Authentication

You can choose to play the game in Guest Mode (where progress is stored locally on your device) or by logging in to save your progress:

  • Android: We use Google Play Games Services for authentication.

  • iOS: We use Unity Player Login for authentication.

When you log in, we receive a unique identifier associated with your account from these services to save and load your game data. We do not receive your email address or password.

C. Data Collected by Third-Party Services

We use third-party services for analytics, crash reporting, and advertising. These services may collect information used to identify you, such as your IP address, device identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID, Apple IDFA), and usage data.

We use the following third-party service providers:

  • Analytics & Stability: Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics

  • Advertising Networks: Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Meta (Facebook) Audience Network, ironSource

These third parties have their own Privacy Policies addressing how they handle such data.

3. Advertising and Personalization

Our App is supported by advertisements. The third-party ad networks listed above may use cookies, mobile advertising identifiers, and other technologies to collect data about your behavior within the App and across other apps/websites to serve you personalized ads.

  • Opting Out of Personalized Ads: You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings.

    • Android: Go to Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization (or "Delete advertising ID").

    • iOS: Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > and disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track".

4. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

Bus Simulator Kerala offers in-app purchases (for in-game currency) and recurring subscriptions (for an ad-free experience).

All transactions and payment details are securely processed entirely through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We do not collect or store your credit card numbers or billing information. We only collect the order receipt ID to verify the legitimacy of the purchase.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

The Service is restricted to users who are 13 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from children without verification of parental consent, we take steps to remove that information from our servers.

6. Global Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you have specific rights regarding your personal data under regional laws:

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA) or the UK, you have the right to:

  • Rectification: Have your information corrected if it is inaccurate.

  • Object: Object to our processing of your personal data.

  • Restriction: Request that we restrict the processing of your personal data.

  • Data Portability: Receive a copy of your personal data in a structured, machine-readable format.

  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time where we relied on your consent to process your information.

Legal Basis for Processing: We process data based on your consent (for personalized ads) and legitimate interests (to ensure the App functions properly via crash logs, device data, and fraud prevention).

Users in Canada and Australia have the right to request access to their personal information and request corrections to it. We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from misuse, interference, loss, unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure in accordance with these acts.
