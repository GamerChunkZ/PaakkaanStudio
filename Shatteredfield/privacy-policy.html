Shatteredfield – Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 18 November 2026 · Applies to the Shatteredfield game for Android (and iOS, where noted)

In short:
  • Shatteredfield has no account of its own and never asks for your name, email or a password. On Android it signs you in with your Google Play Games profile, which belongs to Google, and saves a copy of your progress in Play Games Saved Games. Your best time goes on the Play Games leaderboard, and our awards show as Play Games achievements (section 4).
  • We use Firebase (Google) for gameplay statistics, crash reports and remote game settings (section 5). Gameplay statistics need your consent where the GDPR applies, are never collected for players under 18, and can be turned off in Settings ▸ Account & data.
  • The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads you choose to watch (for example double coins or a revive), and a short ad between some runs.
  • When you first play, we ask your year of birth. We keep only whether you are under 18 or not, never the year. Players under 18 get only child-directed, non-personalised ads and no gameplay statistics.
  • Ads are personalised only if you allow it: on iPhone and iPad through Apple's tracking prompt, and in the EU, UK and Switzerland through a consent form. You can change your choice at any time.
  • Purchases (coin packs and Remove ads) go through Google Play or the App Store. We never see card or bank details.
  • You can delete your data at any time: see Delete your data. We do not sell your information, and we do not use precise location, contacts, camera or microphone.

2. Who the game is for

Shatteredfield is an arcade space shooter for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not designed for or directed at children under 13. Because players may be younger than 18, the game asks for your year of birth the first time you play (section 6) and treats anyone under 18 as a child for advertising and gameplay statistics.

6. Age check and privacy choices

Before any ad is requested or any gameplay statistics are collected, the game goes through these steps in this order:

  1. Age check (first launch only). You enter your year of birth on a keypad. Nothing is pre-filled. If you are under 18, the game stops here: you are not shown any tracking or consent prompt, gameplay statistics stay off, and Google AdMob is told to serve only child-directed, non-personalised ads (section 7).
  2. Apple tracking prompt (iPhone and iPad only). Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt asks whether the game may track you across other companies' apps and websites. It is shown before anything else about privacy. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the game does not access your device's advertising identifier, and you get only non-personalised ads.
  3. Consent where the law requires it. The game asks Google's consent tool whether a consent request applies where you are (currently the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland). This check shows nothing on screen.
    • If it applies and tracking is allowed (Android, or you allowed tracking on iOS), you see a consent form provided by Google, where you can accept or refuse personalised advertising and related purposes.
    • If it applies but you asked iOS not to track you, you see no consent form. Instead, a one-time notice confirms that the game does not track you, and only non-personalised ads are shown.
    • If it applies, the game then asks whether you want to share gameplay statistics (section 5). They stay off unless you say yes.
    • If it does not apply where you are, nothing else is shown.
    • If the check cannot be completed (for example when you are offline), nothing is shown, ads are non-personalised and gameplay statistics stay off until the next time you open the game.

Changing your choices. Where a consent form applies, open Settings ▸ Ad privacy ▸ Change in the game at any time. Gameplay statistics are switched in Settings ▸ Account & data. On iPhone and iPad you can change tracking in Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking. On Android you can delete or reset your advertising ID in Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads (or Settings ▸ Google ▸ Ads).

7. Advertising

Shatteredfield is free because it shows ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, and Google's User Messaging Platform shows the consent form in section 6. If we later add other ad networks through AdMob, we will list them here first.

When an ad is requested or shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects information directly from your device. It can include:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android; on iOS the IDFA only if you allowed tracking) and the app set ID;
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location (country or city, not precise location);
  • device and app information: device model, operating system and version, language, time zone, network type, screen size and the game's version, and diagnostics such as app launch time;
  • ad interactions: which ads were shown, whether you watched them to the end or tapped them, and whether a reward was given;
  • the privacy signals the game passes on: whether you are under 18, whether you consented, and whether personal information may be sold or shared.

Your progress, Play Games profile and gameplay statistics are not given to AdMob.

Google uses this information to deliver ads, to limit how often you see the same ad, to measure ads, to prevent fraud and, only where you have allowed it, to show ads based on your interests. For these purposes Google acts as an independent controller under its own privacy policy:

8. How ads are shown

  • Rewarded ads are always your choice. You decide to watch one to get a reward: double coins after a run, a revive once per run, a daily coin gift, or progress toward unlocking a ship. You never have to watch one to play.
  • Ad breaks (full-screen ads) appear only between runs, never during play, never in your first session, and at most once every few runs. A short countdown appears first.
  • Remove ads is a one-time purchase that stops ad breaks. Rewarded ads stay available because you choose them.

9. Purchases

You can buy coin packs and Remove ads. Purchases are made and paid through Google Play or the Apple App Store, which handle payment and hold your payment details under their own privacy policies. We receive only a confirmation of the purchase (product, order or transaction ID, date). The game records what you bought in your save, which is copied to Saved Games (section 4), and, if gameplay statistics are on, sends a purchase event with the product and price. We never see your name, card or bank details.

10. Store crash reports and the game engine

Besides Firebase Crashlytics (section 5), Google Play (through "Android vitals") or Apple (through App Store Connect) may give us crash reports, but only if you have chosen, in your device's settings, to share usage and diagnostics data with app developers. These reports reach us anonymised and contain technical details such as the device model, operating system version and the part of the game that failed. We use them only to fix problems.

The game is built with the Unity engine, which may send limited technical device information (such as device model, operating system, graphics hardware and screen size) to Unity to improve its engine. It does not include your name, contact details or precise location. Unity privacy policy

11. What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location, and Play Games does not give us your email address.
  • We do not run an account system, chat or social features of our own.
  • We do not sell your personal information. Where personalised ads are allowed, sharing information with Google for that purpose may count as "sharing" under some US state laws; you can stop it as described in sections 6 and 13.
  • We do not use notifications.

12. Where information goes, and how long it is kept

  • On your device: until you delete the game, clear its storage, or use DELETE MY DATA.
  • In Google Play Games: your Saved Games copy is kept by Google with your Google account until DELETE MY DATA deletes it or you delete the game's Play Games data; leaderboard scores and achievements until you delete the game's Play Games data or your Play Games profile (section 4).
  • With Firebase: for the periods in section 5.
  • With Google AdMob: processed on Google's servers and kept for the periods in Google's privacy policy.
  • With us: we see gameplay statistics and crash reports in the Firebase console, the leaderboard in the Play Console, and anonymised store crash reports (section 10). Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer them, and sales records we need for tax as long as tax law requires.

Google's servers may be outside your country, including in the United States. Where information leaves the EU, the UK or another region with transfer rules, Google uses the legal safeguards those rules require, such as Standard Contractual Clauses.
