SHATTERED FIELD

Paakkaan Studio · Arcade · Android

Break the rocks. Outfly the raiders. Survive the Shattered Field.

A fast top-down space shooter you play with one hand: drag to fly, your guns fire on their own, and you tap only to use your ship's ability. The field gets harder the longer you last, so every second counts.

A FIELD THAT FIGHTS BACK

Every run starts calm and ends in chaos. How long can you last?

Rocks that split

Asteroids break into smaller pieces. Iron rocks take a beating, crystal rocks pay out extra coins and volatile rocks go off in chain reactions.

Raiders that learn

Scouts, gunners, snipers, bombers, kamikaze chargers and drone hives join the fight, and they pick up new tricks the longer you survive.

Face the Warden

Every two minutes the Warden arrives. Shoot out its turrets, hit its weak core and crack it open before it retreats, then grab its supply crate.

Perks every level

Collect XP to level up mid-run and pick one of three perk cards: more damage, faster fire, piercing rounds, shrapnel, salvage and more.

Six ships, nine modules

Dart, Fan, Lancer, Bastion, Carrier and Ghost each fly with their own weapon and ability. Upgrade them in the hangar and fit shields, missile pods, drones and more.

A new week, a new field

A weekly modifier changes the rules: dense asteroid fields, raider swarms, comet storms, minefields, bullet curtains and more.

GOOD TO KNOW

Free to play. Optional rewarded ads, short ads between some runs, and in-app purchases (coin packs and Remove ads).
No account needed. Sign in with Google Play Games to keep your progress in the cloud, earn achievements and climb the Best time leaderboard.
Plays offline. No internet connection is needed to play.
Your choice on ads. Ads are personalised only if you allow it, and players under 18 only get non-personalised ads.
