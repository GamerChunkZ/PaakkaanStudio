Shatteredfield – Delete your data

Last updated: 18 November 2026 · Shatteredfield for Android, by AJAS M M (Paakkaan Studio)

Shatteredfield has no account of its own. You sign in with your Google Play Games profile, which belongs to Google. You can delete your Shatteredfield data at any time: your progress in the game, your Play Games profile through Google, and the gameplay statistics and crash reports by email. You do not need the game installed to ask by email.

1. In the game: your progress

  1. Open Shatteredfield and tap the gear on the main menu to open Settings.
  2. Tap Account & data.
  3. Tap DELETE MY DATA, read what will be lost, and confirm.

This straight away:

  • deletes your cloud save (the copy of your progress in Play Games Saved Games);
  • wipes the progress saved on the device (coins, ships, upgrades, modules, awards and statistics). Your sound, control and privacy settings stay;
  • resets your analytics ID, so new gameplay statistics are no longer linked to your earlier play.

The game then returns to its first screen. Deleting the cloud save needs an internet connection.

2. Through Google: your Play Games profile, achievements and scores

Your Play Games profile, gamer name, achievements and leaderboard scores are held by Google in your Play Games account, not by us. You can delete Shatteredfield's Play Games data, or your whole Play Games profile, in the Play Games settings: see Google's help page Delete your Play Games data.

3. By email: gameplay statistics and crash reports

Gameplay statistics (Firebase Analytics) and crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) are linked to a random ID for your copy of the game, not to your name. To have them deleted, send an email to paakkaanstudio@gmail.com with:

  • the subject "Shatteredfield data deletion";
  • your app instance ID, shown on the game's Settings ▸ Account & data sheet. If you already used DELETE MY DATA, the ID shown is the new one; send it anyway and tell us roughly when you deleted, and we will do our best to find the earlier data.

Email us now

We ask Google to delete the data linked to that ID within 30 days and reply to your email when it is done. We never ask for a password. If you can no longer open the game, email us anyway; the statistics expire by themselves (see the table below).

Only want some data deleted?

Email us at the same address and tell us what to remove, and we will delete just that within 30 days where it is in our control.
